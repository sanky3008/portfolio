kaun hai? · support

kaun hai? is a daily guessing game: one famous indian, sixteen faces, and yes or no questions to find them. a new face every day at midnight india time, the same one for everyone.

how to play

  • ask yes or no questions in the box. haan is yes, nahi is no, shayad is "it depends" and doesn't count.
  • tap a card to knock it down when it can't be them. tap again to bring it back.
  • press and hold a card to guess. three wrong guesses and the day is over.

an answer looks wrong

open your questions from the board and tap report on that answer. a person checks it, and if the answer was wrong, it's fixed for everyone and stops counting against you.

help, feedback, deleting your data

email phadnis.sankalp691@gmail.com, or use settings → send feedback in the app. to delete everything, use settings → delete my account and data. more in the privacy policy.
